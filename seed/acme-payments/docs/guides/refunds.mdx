# Refunds

Refund all or part of a charge with `POST /v1/refunds`.

## Choosing a reason

Set `reason` so your finance team can reconcile refunds:

- `requested_by_customer`: the customer asked for their money back.
- `fraudulent`: you believe the charge was fraudulent.
- `duplicate_charge`: the customer was charged twice by mistake.

```python
import acmepay

acmepay.Refund.create(
    charge="ch_3Nf9",
    reason="duplicate_charge",
)
```
